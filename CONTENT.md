## 5. GLOBAL CHECKOUT

**Eyebrow:** Global Checkout

**H2:** Unify payments and fulfillment.
Scale everywhere, convert anywhere.

**Lead:** Eliminate checkout friction globally. Route payments through local acquirers and automate last-mile fulfillment.

**01 · Local Payment Orchestration**
Dynamic routing to regional acquiring banks to maximize authorization rates and eliminate foreign transaction fees.

**02 · Smart Last-Mile Logistics**
Real-time carrier selection, localized shipping rules, and automated landed-cost calculations right at checkout.

**03 · Adaptive Funnel & Localized Experience**
Instant front-end customization for currency, language, and regional checkout preferences.

**04 · Agentic Execution Engine**
Native support for AI buyer agents (machine-to-machine commerce), tokenized automated checkouts, and programmatic cart settlements.

**CTA:** Deploy Next-Gen Checkout → `#contact`
